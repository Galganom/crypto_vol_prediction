\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper has asked whether irregularly arriving cryptocurrency news
can be represented by article-specific magnitudes and temporal decay
profiles that improve short-horizon volatility forecasts. NED maps
a fixed text embedding into these two quantities, sums their decaying
signals, and learns a bounded residual correction to a time-series
variance forecast. The staged training procedure preserves a common
no-news baseline and makes the incremental forecast comparison explicit.

Across six expanding-window test periods, the main model improves
Bitcoin QLIKE by 7.38\% at one hour and 10.83\% at four hours,
alongside smaller but significant reductions in squared log error.
The gains are present in every BTC test window and are strongest
in the tails of the subsequent return distribution. For Ethereum,
average QLIKE improvements are smaller, reverse in the 2025 windows,
and are not accompanied by significant log-MSE improvements.
The contrast illustrates that the value of a news correction depends
on the asset, period, and evaluation loss.

The same qualification applies to the choice of baseline. Adding a
news residual to EGARCH improves log-MSE more than adding it to the
stronger neural baseline, but worsens QLIKE. NED has the lowest QLIKE
among the evaluated models for BTC four-hour forecasts; standalone
EGARCH retains that position in the other three asset--horizon
settings. These results favor evaluating news augmentation through
several complementary measures rather than treating it as a uniform
improvement in forecast quality.

The learned parameters also offer a useful description of the model's
response to text. Most BTC four-hour article signals have short
half-lives, with a median of 42.7 minutes, while a smaller group
persists for several hours. Larger signals tend to decay more slowly,
and negative sentiment is associated with larger magnitudes than
positive sentiment. Macro news has the largest mean magnitude and
longest median half-life among the event categories. The March 2024
episode adds context to these patterns. Their interpretation remains
conditional on the residual loading and calibration, and on the
limits of automated filtering and classification.

Several limitations suggest directions for further work. The news
corpus comes from one English-language publisher and has unequal
coverage of the two assets. Broader sources would help assess whether
the cross-asset difference persists. Training labels overlap the
validation boundary, and the current date bounds omit most of the
last day of each evaluation period; an embargoed design with complete
calendar endpoints would strengthen the chronology. The price features
and news cutoff also use different points within the same bar, which
should be reconciled in a deployment-oriented timing study. Finally,
the single-seed ablations do not quantify optimization variability,
and the interpretable signal decomposition does not identify causal
news effects. Multiple-seed comparisons, placebo news assignments,
and an intercept-only residual control would help distinguish
content-specific information from general forecast recalibration.

The contribution of NED is to make article timing and persistence
explicit parts of a volatility forecast while retaining a compact
description of the news correction. The evidence supports that
mechanism for Bitcoin in the evaluated sample and defines the
conditions under which its apparent advantages require further
testing.
